## Repository routing

- Read `Vision.md`, `Project_milestones.md` and `PROGRESS.md` for current direction and state.
- Use `docs/architecture.md` for architecture placement and `docs/01 AUDIT/AS_IS.md` for verified structure.
- Use `docs/agent-workflow/TASK_LIFECYCLE.md` for the Planner → Implementer → Tester → Reviewer → Closeout handoffs.
- Preserve private replay sources, exact evaluator materials and held-out evidence outside this public candidate repository.
- Keep source organization proportional to implemented work; missions own decisions, research domains own reusable knowledge and experiments own measurements.

Do not infer research capability, hardware qualification or historical task success from structural checks alone.

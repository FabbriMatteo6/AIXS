# Code of Conduct

AIXS is a community-led research project. We want technically serious disagreement without personal hostility.

This project follows the principles of the **Contributor Covenant 2.1**: participation should be welcoming, respectful and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Critique claims, measurements and methods — not people.
- State uncertainty and distinguish evidence from opinion.
- Give credit for prior work and contributions.
- Make room for motivated beginners while maintaining research standards.
- Do not use discriminatory, threatening, sexualized or harassing language or behavior.
- Do not publish another person's private information without permission.

## Research disagreements

Strong disagreement is normal. When evidence conflicts, prefer a reproducible experiment, a clearer benchmark, or an explicit statement that the result remains unresolved.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and restrict participation when behavior makes the project unsafe or persistently unproductive.

For conduct concerns, contact a maintainer privately rather than escalating through a public thread when doing so could expose sensitive information.

Contributor Covenant reference: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

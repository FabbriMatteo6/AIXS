No model is selected yet.

## Candidate criteria

Evaluate candidates using evidence rather than popularity alone:

- open-weight availability and practical access;
- frontier-class capability relative to the intended research question;
- Mixture-of-Experts architecture and inspectable routing/expert structure;
- reproducible tokenizer/config/weight revisions;
- realistic local execution path with existing open tooling;
- quantization and format support;
- ability to measure quality degradation and system behavior;
- storage/RAM/VRAM requirements;
- licensing constraints relevant to research and redistribution;
- healthy upstream ecosystem or enough technical visibility to work independently.
